About Reborro

Reborro is a simple and private tool for tracking borrowed items and borrowed money. People often lend tools, books, chargers, lunch boxes, or even cash, but forget who took them or when they should be returned. Reborro helps you keep a clear record of every item with due dates, notes, and return status.

The app also supports recurring payments such as monthly rent, utility contributions, and weekly dues. You can set an item to repeat automatically, and Reborro creates the next cycle when it’s completed. Useful for students, roommates, families, and shared living.

Reborro works offline and stores all information on your device. No accounts and no cloud systems — complete privacy. You can send WhatsApp reminders with a tap, making it easy to remind someone politely when something is due.
