About Reborro

Reborro is a simple and private tool for tracking borrowed items and borrowed money. People often lend tools, books, chargers, lunch boxes, and even cash, but forget who took them or when they should be returned. Reborro keeps a clean record of every item, including due dates, notes, and return status, helping you stay organised without stress.

The app also supports recurring payments such as monthly rent, utility contributions, shared household payments, or weekly dues. You can set an item to repeat automatically, and Reborro will create the next cycle once it is completed. This makes it useful for students, roommates, families, and anyone who manages small recurring obligations.

Reborro works offline and stores all your data on your device. No account, no cloud storage, no tracking — complete privacy. You can also send WhatsApp reminders with a single tap, making it easy to remind someone politely without awkward conversations.
