Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2025

Reborro is a simple tool to help you track borrowed items and money.

We respect your privacy. The app is designed so that your data never leaves your device.

1. Data We Collect

Reborro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

We do not use a database, login system, cloud storage, or external APIs for your personal information.

All data you add inside the app (names, items, dates, notes, amounts) is stored locally on your own device, using browser storage.

2. Backups

If you choose to export a backup file, it is created locally and saved directly to your device or shared through apps you personally select (e.g., WhatsApp, email).

Reborro never receives a copy of your backup.

If you import a backup file, it is processed entirely on your device.

3. No Tracking

Reborro does not use:

  • cookies
  • behavioural tracking
  • advertising trackers
  • third-party SDKs
  • fingerprinting
  • analytics tied to personal identity

We may use privacy-friendly, aggregate analytics (e.g., Cloudflare) on our website to understand traffic patterns, but this data is anonymous and not linked to app usage.

4. Permissions

Reborro does not request access to contacts, photos, or device files.

Manual phone number entry is optional and stays on your device.

5. Third-Party Services

Reborro may use common web hosting and CDN services to deliver the site, such as Cloudflare. These services may collect anonymized technical logs (e.g., IP used for routing). We do not link these logs to any personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

Reborro does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Because there is no account and no data is sent to us, the app is safe for all ages.

7. Changes

We may update this policy if features change. Updates will be posted on this page.

8. Contact

If you have any questions, you can contact us at:

support@reborro.com
